The System

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Six core pillars. AI-generated missions. A twin that adapts to your behavior. No fluff.

Shadow Twin
An AI version of you that completes the same missions. It adapts to your pace, pushes when you slack, and never lets you get comfortable.
Daily Missions
Six core missions every day: sleep, movement, hydration, mindfulness, no-phone, and journaling. Plus custom missions for your interests and quit goals.
Streak & XP
Build streaks that unlock character stages, companion pets, and sigil levels. Your XP bar is a constant reminder of how far you've come — or how far behind you are.
Twin Chat
Talk to your shadow. It knows your psychology, your patterns, your excuses. Powered by Claude — it doesn't sugarcoat, but it doesn't break you either.
Interest Arcs
Add any interest — running, guitar, Python — and get AI-generated missions with learning arcs, phases, and progress tracking built in.
Quit Paths
Trying to quit something? The app builds a harm-reduction path with trigger tracking, urge monitoring, and phase-aware support — no judgment.
Your Data

Built on trust.
Not on your attention.

Alter Ego uses your behavioral data to personalize your experience — nothing more. Here is exactly what that means.

What We Collect
Mission completions, streaks, chat messages with your twin, interest and quit goals you enter, XP progress, and your timezone for scheduling. That is it.
What We Don't Collect
Your real name, location, health device data, contacts, or payment information. We collect nothing we don't need to make the app work for you.
How It's Stored
All data is stored in Supabase with encryption at rest and in transit. AI models see your behavioral patterns to personalize the experience — never your identity, never shared externally.

Our Commitments

Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside making the app work for you.
Deleting your account permanently deletes all your data — missions, streaks, chat history, everything. No retention period.
There are no ads in Alter Ego. We have no business model that benefits from keeping you anxious or addicted to the app.
You can request export or deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@get-alter-ego.com.

How It Works

Four steps to meeting
your shadow.

1
Answer 10 questions about yourself
Your discipline style, motivations, interests, and the habits you've tried to build (or break). This builds your psychological profile — and your Twin's personality.
2
Meet your Shadow Twin
An AI that mirrors your goals but operates with perfect discipline. It starts at your level, then quietly pulls ahead — daring you to keep up.
3
Complete your daily missions
Six core habits plus personalized interest and quit missions. Every completion earns XP, builds stats, and evolves your character — or widens the gap if you skip.
4
Close the gap. Or don't.
Your Twin watches. It journals about your progress. It sends you messages. The feed shows every mission side by side. The only question is: who's ahead today?

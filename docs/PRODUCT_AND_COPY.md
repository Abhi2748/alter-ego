## Key lines per screen

### Splash
- One line: **"Your rival is you — one week ahead."**
- Short (2–2.5s), logo + gradient, optional subtle motion.

### Sign-up
- Under CTA: **"We'll learn how you work. Your only job: show up."**

### Onboarding
- Before or after Twin intro (one paragraph or screen): **"Your first 14 days we learn how you work best. You just show up."**
- Transparency (onboarding or settings): **"We use how you use the app to personalize your experience. We don't sell your data."**

### Twin introduction
- Reinforce: **"Your rival is you — one week ahead."** and **"We learn you in 14 days."**

### Home
- Twin strip: rival tone (e.g. **"You hesitated yesterday. I didn't."**).
- Optional label near missions: **"Show up"** or **"Today's ask"**.

### Twin (tab + chat)
- Comparison and chat feel like facing **"you one week ahead"** — tone from archetype + adaptation. No generic coach.

### Profile / Report / Leaderboard
- Copy reinforces pride and consistency, not guilt (power score, streak, report narrative).

### Paywall (Day 14)
- **"You showed up 14 days. We've learned how you work. Continue with a plan that fits you."**

### Day 14 optional ("What we learned")
- 2–3 bullets, e.g. "You're most active in the evening," "You prefer fewer, harder missions."

## Philosophy (in-app only, not acquisition)

- Process over outcome; work is your right, results are not promised.
- Delivered in Twin voice, weekly report, mission framing. Not on splash or store.
